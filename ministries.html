Children's Church Ministry

Bringing children to Christ and training them to walk in the power of the Holy Spirit

Ages: Preschool (3-5) & Elementary/Middle School (6-12)
When: During Sunday Worship Service
Where: Children's Ministry Area

Our Children's Church mission is to bring children to Christ and train them to walk and minister in the power of the Holy Spirit. We focus on exposing our children to the total worship experience which will prepare them for participating in the worship service in the church sanctuary.

Programs Include:

  • Nursery/Pre-School: Safe, loving care for our youngest members
  • Preschool (Ages 3-5): Bible stories, songs, and hands-on activities
  • Elementary/Middle School (Ages 6-12): Dynamic lessons and worship participation
  • Summer/Eagle Enrichment Program: Educational and spiritual development during summer

The children participate in singing, prayer, scripture, and lessons. Activities are incorporated to stimulate each child's participation.

Youth Group / Warriors for Christ

Empowering the next generation to live boldly for Christ

Ages: Middle & High School
When: Sundays & Wednesdays

Our Youth Group, known as Warriors for Christ, creates a space where teenagers can ask tough questions, build authentic friendships, and develop a faith that's their own. We're committed to walking alongside teens as they navigate the challenges of adolescence with biblical truth and grace.

Youth Programs:

  • Warriors for Christ: Youth group meetings and activities
  • Warriors for Christ Praise Choir / Junior Choir: Musical ministry for youth
  • Young Adult Ministry Serving (YAMS): Ministry opportunities for young adults
  • FBC Mentoring Ministry: Mentorship and guidance for youth development
  • Boy Scouts: Character-building activities and community service

Music Ministry

Leading hearts in praise and adoration

Director: Bro. Frederick Williams

Music is a vital part of our worship experience at Friendship Baptist. Whether you sing, play an instrument, or work behind the scenes, there's a place for you to use your gifts to glorify God and minister to others.

Choirs & Musical Groups:

  • Senior Choir: Traditional choir with anthems and special music
  • A.B. Miles Voices of Praise: Praise and worship ensemble
  • John Phillips Choir: Named in honor of our founding pastor
  • Friendship Male Chorus: Men's vocal ministry
  • Junior Choir / Warriors for Christ Praise Choir: Youth musical ministry
  • Friendship Baptist Church Pipers: Instrumental ensemble
  • Ascension Praise Dancers: Worship through sacred dance (organized in 2003)

Supporting Ministries:

  • Audio Ministry: Technical support for worship services

Community & Outreach Ministries

Serving locally and reaching our community with Christ's love

Friendship Baptist Church routinely has community-related information that is vital to the community that we serve. We believe the Gospel compels us to action - caring for the poor, sharing good news, and demonstrating God's love through practical service.

Community Ministries:

  • Friendship Community Improvement Corp. (FCIC): Community development initiatives
  • Shoe Box Ministry: Providing essential items to those in need
  • Labuda Doll Ministry: Creating comfort dolls for children
  • Van/Transportation Ministry: Providing transportation for members and community
  • Hospitality Ministry: Welcoming visitors and serving at church events
  • Kitchen Ministry: Preparing meals for church functions and community events

Christian Education Ministry

Growing deeper in God's Word together

Sunday School: 9:00 AM
Wednesday Bible Study: 11:45 AM & 6:15 PM

"Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needed not to be ashamed, rightly diving the word of truth." - II Timothy 2:15 (KJV). Spiritual growth happens best in community. Our Bible studies provide settings where you can ask questions, share life, and apply Scripture to everyday challenges.

Educational Ministries:

  • Sunday School Ministry: Bible study classes for all ages at 9:00 AM
  • Wednesday Morning Bible Study: Midday study at 11:45 AM
  • Wednesday Prayer Service: Corporate prayer at 5:45 PM
  • Wednesday Evening Bible Study: In-depth study at 6:15 PM
  • Christian Education Ministry: Educational programming and curriculum
  • Church Council Ministry: Leadership training and development
