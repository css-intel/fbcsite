Greetings from Rev. Clinton "T.C." Edwards, Jr., M.Div.

Blessings to you in the Name of our Lord Jesus Christ!

Here at Friendship Baptist Church in Aiken, SC, we seek to carry out the Great Commission of Jesus Christ, to promote Christian fellowship, spiritual growth among its members, Bible study, instruction in Bible Doctrine, and to combine the efforts of individual members in evangelism, mission, education, and benevolence.

Here, we are a family – not always perfect, but still bound together by something more significant than we are. Centered on God's Word, and empowered by His Holy Spirit, together we seek to be the kind of congregation our Lord has called us to be. And we would love to have you join us!

We would love to welcome you in worship; we would love for you to come get to know our family so that we may worship and serve the Lord together.

Yours In Christ,
Reverend Clinton Edwards, Jr., M.Div.
First Lady Robbin K. Edwards
